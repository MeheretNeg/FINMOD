# 🎓 Ethiopian School Financial Modeling App

## Interactive Web Application - User Guide

### 📊 Overview

This is a **user-friendly web application** that allows anyone - even without finance or accounting background - to create accurate financial feasibility studies for schools in Ethiopia.

**Built on RESEARCH & INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS:**
- ✅ International Baccalaureate (IB) guidelines
- ✅ Cambridge International standards
- ✅ Ethiopian Ministry of Education regulations
- ✅ Addis Ababa market research (2024)
- ✅ World Bank education statistics
- ✅ Verified construction and staffing costs

## 📋 How to Use the App

### Tab 1: 📊 Dashboard
**Purpose:** Get a quick overview and instant estimates

**What to do:**
1. Enter basic information (building size, students, tuition)
2. See immediate financial estimates
3. Use this to get a rough idea before detailed planning

**Key Metrics Shown:**
- Total investment needed
- Year 1 revenue estimate
- Years to reach full capacity
- Estimated return on investment

---

### Tab 2: 💰 Investment & Costs
**Purpose:** Plan your construction and equipment costs

**Research-Based Defaults:**
- **Construction costs**: 8,000-25,000 ETB/sqm (based on Ethiopian market)
- **Building size**: Minimum 12 sqm per student (international standard)
- **Equipment costs**: Based on actual vendor quotes

**What to adjust:**
1. **Building Area**: How big will your school be?
   - Small (200-300 students): 3,000-4,000 sqm
   - Medium (300-500 students): 5,000-7,000 sqm
   - Large (500-800 students): 7,000-10,000 sqm

2. **Construction Quality**:
   - Basic: 10,000 ETB/sqm (local materials)
   - Standard: 15,000 ETB/sqm ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
   - Premium: 20,000+ ETB/sqm (international standards)

3. **Financing**:
   - Equity (own money): Minimum 30%, recommended 40%
   - Debt (bank loan): Maximum 70%
   - Interest rates: 13-16% (Ethiopian bank rates)

**⚠️ VALIDATION REQUIRED:**
- Get 3+ contractor quotes before finalizing
- Verify current material costs (inflation impact)
- Check land title and zoning permits

### Tab 5: 📉 Results & Analysis
**Purpose:** See if your school is financially viable

**Key Metrics Explained:**

**Net Present Value (NPV):**
- **Positive NPV = Good Investment** ✅
- **Negative NPV = Reconsider** ❌
- Uses 15% discount rate (Ethiopian investment benchmark)

**Payback Period:**
- **4-6 years = Good** ✅
- **7-9 years = Acceptable** ⚠️
- **10+ years = Too long** ❌

**Year 1 Operating Profit:**
- **Negative** is normal (starting up)
- Should be positive by Year 2-3
- If loss > 50% of revenue in Year 1 = Warning sign ⚠️

**Decision Framework:**

✅ **FINANCIALLY VIABLE** if:
- NPV > 0
- Payback < 7 years
- Year 1 loss < 50% of revenue
- Operating margin reaches 15%+ by Year 5

⚠️ **MARGINAL** if:
- NPV > 0 but small
- Payback 7-9 years
- Slow enrollment growth
- Tight cash flow

❌ **NOT VIABLE** if:
- NPV < 0
- Payback > 10 years
- Unsustainable losses
- Insufficient demand

## 💡 Tips for Accurate Modeling

### 1. Be Conservative with Revenue
- **Don't** assume optimistic enrollment
- **Do** plan for 30-40% capacity in Year 1
- **Remember** parents may delay payments

### 2. Be Realistic with Costs
- **Don't** underestimate construction costs
- **Do** add 10-15% contingency
- **Remember** Ethiopian inflation is 20%+

### 3. Validate Assumptions
- **Every** tuition rate should match market research
- **Every** cost should have a quote or benchmark
- **Every** projection should be conservative

### 4. Consider Risks
- Currency fluctuation (ETB/USD)
- Political/economic instability
- Competition from new schools
- Enrollment uncertainty
- Staff retention challenges

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Problem: NPV is negative**
- Solution: Reduce construction costs, increase tuition, or improve enrollment projections
- Check if assumptions are realistic

**Problem: Payback period too long**
- Solution: Increase Year 1 enrollment, reduce initial investment, or raise fees
- Consider phased construction

**Problem: Large Year 1 losses**
- Solution: Normal for schools, but ensure you have cash reserves
- Plan for 6-12 months operating expenses in reserve

**Problem: App shows "Not Viable"**
- Solution: Don't proceed without major changes
- Reconsider location, target market, or school size
- Seek professional consultation
